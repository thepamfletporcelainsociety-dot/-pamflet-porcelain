Review Policy

Reviews are never bought, traded, or guaranteed. Here is exactly what that means in practice.

No paid or traded reviews

State plainly that the Society does not pay readers for reviews, does not exchange reviews between authors, and does not guarantee a star rating or review count as part of any curation series.

Reader discretion

Explain that a review is entirely optional for a reader who finishes a book, is written unedited in the reader's own words, and is posted on the platform of the reader's choosing.

What authors can expect

Set expectations for how many of the twenty salon readers might choose to review, and how that differs from the structured feedback and salon transcript, which every author receives regardless.
