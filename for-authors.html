Who the Society is for

Independent and small-press authors who want considered, structured feedback from readers who actually finish the book — not a review farm, not a marketing service, and not a guarantee of stars.

What's included

Every author accepted into a series receives the full four-movement process: submission and reading, structured feedback against a fixed rubric, a moderated discussion salon, and the option for readers to post their own honest, unpaid reviews.

Twelve titles per series

Each six-week series accepts twelve titles so every book receives full attention from curators and readers alike. Describe your eligibility criteria, genres accepted, and pricing here.
