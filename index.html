The process

How curation works

Four movements across six weeks. Every author receives the same sequence, and every reader participates on their own terms.

I

Submission & Reading

You send the manuscript or finished edition. Two curators read it in full and place it with the readers most likely to love it.

II

Structured Feedback

Readers respond to a fixed rubric — voice, pacing, character, ending — so what returns to you is usable, not vague.

III

Discussion Salon

A moderated evening with twenty readers, transcribed and delivered to you with the quotes worth keeping.

IV

Honest Reviews

Readers who finish the book may post their own unedited opinion, entirely at their discretion, on the platform of their choosing.
